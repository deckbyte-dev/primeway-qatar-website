1. We read your message and call or message you back.
2. We ask a few questions about the business and the owners.
3. You get a written plan with documents, steps and cost.

## Before you call, it helps to have

- The type of business you want to do, in plain words
- Who the owners will be and where they live
- Copies of passports and, for people in Qatar, Qatar IDs
- If you already have a company: the commercial registration and licence
