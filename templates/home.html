Who is behind Primeway

Primeway for Trading and Services is a Qatar based company that does government paperwork for other companies.

We started because small business owners kept losing weeks to simple mistakes: a wrong activity code, a document that was not attested, a renewal that was missed by two days. Our job is to prevent those.

  • One person on your file from the first call to the last card. You do not get passed around.

  • A list of documents on day one, so you only collect things once.
