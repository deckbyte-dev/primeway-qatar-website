The limited liability company, often written WLL in Qatar, is the most common way to run a business here. It lets you sell to customers anywhere in the country and it limits your personal risk to the capital you put in. This page explains how it works and how we set one up. For the full picture of options, start at [company formation in Qatar](/company-formation-qatar/)

## Who an LLC suits

- Traders, contractors and service businesses that serve customers across Qatar
- Businesses that want to bid for private sector contracts
- Foreign investors who want full ownership in an allowed sector (see [100 percent foreign ownership](/company-formation-qatar/100-percent-foreign-ownership/))
- Partners who want a clear legal split of shares and roles

## Ownership

An LLC has at least two shareholders in some structures and can be owned by one person in others. Foreign shareholders may hold up to 100 percent in most sectors with ministry approval. If your activity needs a Qatari partner, we explain the options. See: [Qatari partner or sponsor section](/company-formation-qatar/#do-you-need-a-qatari-partner-or-sponsor)

## Capital

There is no fixed general minimum capital for most LLCs today. The old figure of QAR 200,000 is no longer the rule. Your activity, your bank and your lease can still ask for more, so we set the capital based on what the business needs, not on a number from an old article.

## How we set it up

1. Check that your activity can be licensed and whether another authority must approve it.
2. Reserve the trade name. Names have rules. A name made of generic words alone will usually be refused.
3. Prepare the memorandum of association and sign it, digitally through Single Window or with a notary.
4. Register in the commercial register and get the CR.
5. Issue the commercial licence once the premises are confirmed.
6. Receive the establishment registration card.
7. Register for tax with the General Tax Authority and receive the tax card through Dhareeba.
8. Open the corporate bank account and start trading.

## Documents

- Passports of all shareholders, and Qatar IDs for residents
- Details of the activity and the proposed trade names
- Lease or tenancy details for the office
- For a shareholder that is a company: its incorporation papers, attested if issued abroad
- Power of attorney if someone signs on your behalf

## Questions about LLCs

**Q: What does WLL mean?**

It stands for "With Limited Liability". In everyday use, WLL and LLC mean the same thing in Qatar.

**Q: Can a foreigner open an LLC without a Qatari partner?**

In most sectors, yes, with approval from the Ministry of Commerce and Industry under Law No. 1 of 2019. Restricted sectors still have their own rules.

**Q: LLC or free zone?**

If most of your customers are inside Qatar, the mainland LLC is usually the right fit. If you export or store goods, a free zone may be cheaper. We compare both for you. Read: [Free zone and QFC sections](/company-formation-qatar/#free-zone-company-in-qatar)

**Q: How long does an LLC take?**

With papers ready, a few weeks is common. Attestations, extra approvals and premises can add time. [CONFIRM: range]

**Q: Can I add activities later?**

Yes, through an amendment to the CR and licence. Each extra activity has its own fee and sometimes its own approval.
