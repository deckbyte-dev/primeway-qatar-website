Can a foreigner own 100 percent of a company in Qatar?

In most sectors, yes. Since Law No. 1 of 2019, foreign investors can hold full ownership of a company with approval from the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. A few sectors, such as banking and insurance, have their own rules. We explain how it works and what the ministry looks at.

Questions we hear often

How long does it take to set up a company in Qatar?

For a standard mainland LLC with all documents ready, it often takes a few weeks from trade name to licence. Delays usually come from missing attestations or activity approvals. We give you a realistic range after we hear your business details.

Do I need to be in Qatar to start a company?

Not for every step. Many steps are online and some can be done by power of attorney. You will need to sign the incorporation papers, either in person, digitally through Single Window or through an authorised signatory. We tell you which applies to you.
