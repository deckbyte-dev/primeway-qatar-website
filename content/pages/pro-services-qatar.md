PRO stands for public relations officer. In Qatar, it means the person who deals with ministries and government portals on behalf of a company. Many small companies do not have enough work for a full time PRO. We give you one when you need it, and you pay for the work done.

## What a PRO handles

- Company registration, licence changes and renewals
- Work visas, residence permits and Qatar IDs for staff
- Labour contracts, transfers, cancellations and exit permits
- Tax card, tax returns and tax clearance
- Qatar Chamber membership and certificates
- Attestation and translation of company documents
- Changes of address, manager, shareholders or activity
- Closing a company properly

## Single Window and MOCI services

Single Window is the online platform where most company registration and licence work is done, together with the Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MOCI). We use it every day. Through it we reserve and renew trade names, register companies, issue and amend commercial licences, add or remove activities, change the manager, shareholders, capital or address, add branches, and cancel a licence when a company closes.

Trade names must be distinctive. Names made only of generic words are usually refused, so we suggest several options. The most common amendments are adding an activity, changing the manager, updating an address and changing shares. Each has its own documents and sometimes a knock on step with the bank or tax authority. We tell you before it surprises you.

A mismatch in a name or number is a common reason for rejection. We cross check every document before we submit.

## Ministry of Labour services

Hiring in Qatar involves the Ministry of Labour at several points. We handle requests for work visa approvals, employment contracts in the required form, transfers of employees from another employer, contract amendments and cancellations, and notices from the labour department.

Hiring usually runs like this: the company has an active licence, establishment card and bank account. It applies for approval to hire from abroad or to transfer someone already in Qatar. The contract is signed. Interior steps follow. Salary is then paid through the Wage Protection System. Exact steps and forms change, so we follow the latest procedure for each case.

Things that cause delay: an expired establishment card, a salary or job title in the contract that does not match the application, no Wage Protection System set up with the bank, or a transfer where the previous employer has not cleared the worker.

The number of employees you can hire depends on company size, premises, activity and the quota rules at the time. We check your position before you recruit.

## Ministry of Interior services

The Ministry of Interior handles entry, residence and identity, mostly through Metrash2 and its service centres. We handle residence permits for employees and family, Qatar ID issue and renewal, business and visit visa requests, establishment registration and renewal, and exit permits where the rule applies.

Every licensed company also has an establishment registration. It identifies the company in the Interior system and is needed before you can sponsor staff. It is issued with a new licence and can be refreshed after the licence is renewed. We keep it current so hiring never stops because of an expired card.

A residence permit usually follows this order: entry on a work visa or a transfer, medical test and fingerprints, residence permit and Qatar ID, then the health card. ## Tax Authority and Dhareeba services

Every company with a commercial registration has to register with the General Tax Authority (GTA) and use its portal, Dhareeba, even if it expects to pay no tax. We handle registration and the tax card, updates after changes in address, activity or owners, submission of the annual return using figures from your accountant or auditor, deadline extension requests, tax clearance certificates and replies to notices.

We do not prepare financial statements or tax computations. We work with your accountant or auditor, or introduce one. [CONFIRM: client's accounting and audit position and any partner firm]

The basics: the standard corporate tax rate under the general regime is 10 percent of taxable profit. Qatar has no personal income tax on salaries and no VAT at the moment. The annual return is usually due within four months of the year end, with extensions announced by the authority from time to time. Some returns need audited accounts. Penalties apply for late filing and late payment. Qatar Financial Centre companies use the QFC's own system.

Without a tax registration you cannot get certain approvals, and banks and customers often ask to see the tax card. We register early, together with company setup.

## Qatar Chamber membership

Most licensed companies have to register with Qatar Chamber, and the membership is renewed every year together with the commercial registration. We handle first registration, yearly renewal, amendments after company changes, Chamber attestation of commercial documents such as invoices, certificates of origin for exporters where required, and Chamber letters for tenders and visas.

The fee depends on the activity and the number of branches. A missed renewal can hold up your licence and other approvals. [CONFIRM: whether the client handles certificates of origin directly]

## Trade licence and CR renewal

A company in Qatar is licensed through several linked documents. If one expires, others can stop working. We renew them together: the commercial registration, the commercial licence, the establishment card, Chamber membership, and any activity approvals.

Single Window has a Comprehensive Renewal service that renews the CR, permit and other eligible linked approvals in one application. Renewal can be for one year or a longer period, up to five years. After the licence is renewed, the establishment registration can be refreshed and checked in Metrash.

What blocks renewal: an expired lease or a mismatch of address, a missing tax return, unpaid fines, an activity that now needs an extra approval, or different names or numbers across documents. We check your file and clear what we can. Start a month before the date.

If your licence has already expired, tell us at once. Late renewal brings fines and a long gap can mean starting parts of the process again.

## Company cancellation (liquidation) in Qatar

Company cancellation means closing a company properly, not just letting the licence expire. A company left behind keeps gathering fines, staff records stay open and the owners can be blocked from other business. Proper liquidation closes everything in order.

For a voluntary closure of a company that can pay its debts, the usual steps are: the owners resolve to dissolve the company and appoint a liquidator, the decision is registered in the commercial register, the liquidator publishes the required notice, debts are settled, employees are paid and their contracts and visas are cancelled, tax clearance is obtained, the licence, CR and establishment registration are cancelled, and the bank account is closed.

Common mistakes: letting the licence expire and assuming the company is closed, cancelling visas before paying salary dues, skipping the last tax return, and closing the bank account before final payments.
