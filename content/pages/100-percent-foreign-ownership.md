Many foreign investors still believe they need a Qatari partner who owns 51 percent. That was the old rule. Today, in most sectors, a foreign investor can own the whole company. This page explains how that works.

## The law behind it

Law No. 1 of 2019 on the regulation of foreign capital investment allows non Qatari investors to hold up to 100 percent of a company in most economic sectors. The approval comes from the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. It is not automatic, so the application has to be done properly.

## Which sectors are excluded

The law keeps a few areas outside the general rule. These include banking and insurance, commercial agencies and some activities linked to natural resources. Some other activities can need an extra approval. Before you plan anything, we check your activity against the current list.

## Three ways to own a company fully

1. Mainland LLC with full foreign ownership. For selling inside Qatar. See: [LLC](/company-formation-qatar/llc/)
2. Free zone company. Ownership is open to foreigners inside the zones, with limits on local sales. See: [Free zone section](/company-formation-qatar/#free-zone-company-in-qatar)
3. Qatar Financial Centre company. For financial and professional services. See: [QFC section](/company-formation-qatar/#qatar-financial-centre-qfc-company-setup)

## What the ministry looks at

- The activity and whether it falls in an allowed sector
- Who the owners are, with documents for each
- If the owner is a company, its papers and who controls it
- The premises where the company will operate

We prepare the application so these points are clear on the first submission.

## What you need to give us

- Passport copies of the owners
- Company documents for a corporate owner, attested if issued abroad
- A short description of what the business will do
- The planned office address, or let us help find a fit

## What this means for a Qatari partner

Full foreign ownership means you do not need a sponsor who holds the shares. Some owners still choose a Qatari partner for business reasons, such as contracts with government entities. That is a business choice, not a legal requirement for most sectors. See: [Qatari partner or sponsor section](/company-formation-qatar/#do-you-need-a-qatari-partner-or-sponsor)

## Questions

**Q: Is approval guaranteed?**

No. The ministry decides. A clean, complete application helps and we make sure of that. We also tell you early if your activity is likely to be a problem.

**Q: Do I have to live in Qatar to own the company?**

No. You do need someone who can act as manager, and you will need a residence permit if you plan to live and work here. We explain the options.

**Q: Do taxes change with foreign ownership?**

A company with foreign ownership is generally subject to the 10 percent corporate tax on its Qatar income under the general regime. See: [Tax Authority and Dhareeba section](/pro-services-qatar/#tax-authority-and-dhareeba-services)

**Q: Can I convert an existing company to full foreign ownership?**

Sometimes. It depends on the activity and the current owners. Send us the CR and licence and we will check.
