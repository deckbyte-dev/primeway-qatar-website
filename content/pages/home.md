## Which type of company fits you?

This is the first question and it decides everything after it. Here is the short version.

- LLC on the mainland. The usual choice if you want to sell to customers anywhere in Qatar. Read: [LLC in Qatar](/company-formation-qatar/llc/)
- Free zone company. Good for export, storage and some service businesses, with limits on selling inside Qatar. Read: [Free zone section](/company-formation-qatar/#free-zone-company-in-qatar)
- Qatar Financial Centre (QFC). For financial, professional and some corporate services. Read: [QFC section](/company-formation-qatar/#qatar-financial-centre-qfc-company-setup)
- Representative office. For a foreign company that wants to explore the market, not sell. Read: [Representative office section](/company-formation-qatar/#representative-office-in-qatar)

Not sure? Send us two lines about your business and we will tell you which one fits.

## Can a foreigner own 100 percent of a company in Qatar?

In most sectors, yes. Since Law No. 1 of 2019, foreign investors can hold full ownership of a company with approval from the Ministry of Commerce and Industry. A few sectors, such as banking and insurance, have their own rules. We explain how it works and what the ministry looks at.
