Getting a person into Qatar and into legal work or residence takes two ministries and a few steps in order. We prepare the applications and track them until the Qatar ID is in the person's hand.

## Work visas for company staff

For companies with an active licence and establishment card. We handle the approval request, the contract, entry, medical, fingerprints, residence permit and Qatar ID. See: [Ministry of Labour and Ministry of Interior sections](/pro-services-qatar/#ministry-of-labour-services)

## Family residence

Residents who meet the conditions can sponsor a spouse and children. We prepare the application and the attested documents. The salary and housing conditions for sponsoring family change from time to time, so we confirm them for you. [CONFIRM: current conditions]

## Business and visit visas

For clients or partners who want to visit Qatar for meetings. The company or a hotel can request them in some cases. We tell you which route fits.

[CONFIRM: client to confirm whether they offer business and visit visa requests]

## Transfers and changes

- Moving an employee between employers
- Changing a job title or sponsor details
- Renewing residence permits
- Cancelling a visa when someone leaves

## Documents that often hold things up

- Educational and marriage certificates not attested. See: [document attestation](/document-attestation-qatar/)
- Passport with too little validity
- Photographs that do not meet the format
- Names spelled differently across documents

We check the full set before submission.

## What we need to start

- Passport copy and a recent photograph
- The company CR, licence and establishment card, for company visas
- Attested certificates, where the job or the visa needs them
- Contract or offer letter

## Questions

**Q: How long does a work visa take?**

It depends on the approvals and the person's location. We give a realistic range after we see the file. [CONFIRM: range]

**Q: Can I get a work visa for myself, as an owner?**

Yes, as a partner or manager of a licensed company. We explain the steps.

**Q: Do you do tourist visas for other countries?**

No. We stay with Qatar procedures. [CONFIRM]

**Q: What is a Qatar ID?**

The residence identity card issued after the residence permit. It is used for banking, health care and government services.
