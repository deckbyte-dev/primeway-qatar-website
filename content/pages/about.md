## A short version of who we are

Primeway for Trading and Services is a business service company in Qatar. We help people start companies, keep them licensed and close them when the time comes. Most of our work is the part nobody enjoys: forms, stamps, ministry portals and follow ups.

## Why we do this work

Government procedures in Qatar are clear once you know them. The trouble is that each step depends on the one before it. A wrong activity code at the start can block a licence three weeks later. A certificate that was not attested means the visa application comes back.

We do this every day, so we catch those problems before they cost you time. That is the whole idea of the company.

## How we work

- You deal with one person. That person knows your file.
- We tell you the cost before we begin, and we keep our fee apart from government fees.
- We ask for documents in one list, once.
- We say no when something will not work. It is cheaper for you to hear it on day one.
- We keep your renewal dates and warn you early.
