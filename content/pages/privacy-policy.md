## Who we are

This website is run by Primeway for Trading and Services, Doha, Qatar. When this page says "we", it means that company.

## What we collect

When you fill in a form on this site, we collect what you type: your name, phone number, email address if you give one, the service you are asking about and your message. We also save the page you sent the form from.

If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we keep that conversation.

We do not ask for passport numbers, ID numbers or bank details through the website forms. Please do not type them in. We will tell you how to send documents safely when the time comes.

## Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry, to prepare a quote and to carry out the work you ask us to do. We do not sell your details. We do not send marketing to people who have not asked for it.

## Who sees it

Our team. Form submissions go to a Google Sheet and an email inbox that only our staff can open. These services are run by Google, and your details may be stored on their servers outside Qatar.
