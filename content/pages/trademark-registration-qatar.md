In Qatar, trademark rights come from registration. The first person to file for a name or logo in a class generally gets the right, even if someone else used it earlier. If your brand matters to your business, register it before you promote it.

## What you can register

- A business or product name
- A logo
- A slogan
- A combination of these

Some marks cannot be registered, such as ones that are purely descriptive, misleading or against public order.

## How long protection lasts

A registered trademark is protected for 10 years from the filing date and can be renewed again and again for further 10 year periods. Renewal is due in the last year of protection, with a short grace period after expiry.

## How we file

1. Search the register for marks that clash with yours.
2. Decide the classes. Qatar applications are filed per class, so a brand used on clothes and on food needs two.
3. Prepare the application with the owner details and a clear image of the mark.
4. File it with the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
5. Answer any objection from the examiner.
6. After acceptance, publication and the opposition period.
7. Pay the final fees and receive the registration certificate.

## Timing

If there is no objection, the whole process often runs a year or a little more. If someone opposes or the examiner objects, it takes longer. [CONFIRM: client's usual range]

## Documents

- Name and address of the owner
- Commercial registration, for a company
- Clear image of the mark, in the format requested
- List of goods or services
- Power of attorney in our name
- For a foreign owner: attested incorporation papers and translation. See: [attestation](/document-attestation-qatar/)

## Keep it alive

A mark that is not used for five years in a row can be challenged and removed. Keep using it and keep your renewal date. We store the date and remind you.

## Questions

**Q: Do I need a company to register a trademark?**

No. Individuals can also register. Most business owners register in the company name.

**Q: Does a Qatar trademark cover other countries?**

No. Protection is by country. Qatar applies the GCC trademark law, but registration is still handled nationally. If you plan to sell abroad, ask us about other filings.

**Q: Is my domain name enough?**

No. A domain gives you a web address, not a legal right to the brand.

**Q: Can I register the same name in different fields?**

Yes, in separate classes, each with its own fee.
